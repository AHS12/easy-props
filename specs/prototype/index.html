Color

Whisper commitment: one accent (moss) carries the primary action and selection. Clay marks destructive and attention states. Neutrals are warm-tinted so surfaces read as authored rather than empty.

Typography

Nunito, humanist and warm. Numbers use tabular figures so sliders do not jitter while dragging.

Buttons

Every control that can enter a state owns a design for it. Focus rings are never removed.

Controls

Segmented choices for categorical properties, sliders only for genuinely continuous values. No bare 0-1 ranges.

Feedback

Undo beats confirm. Errors state what broke and what to do next, and never blame the user.

Space, radius, elevation

The 1-4-9 rhythm: every gap is a multiple of 4px or 9px. Shadows are warm-tinted, never black.
